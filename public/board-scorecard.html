Five questions on AI agent security

A one-page scorecard for the board. Ask the same five every quarter and write the answers down.

QuarterDateAsked of

1How many agents run here, and who owns each?

Ask forThe count found running, and an owner per entry. Not the list of approved tools.
StrongA recent inventory built from what is actually on devices and networks, with an owner and a date.
WeakA survey, a policy, or a list of approved tools.
Our answerNumberOwner

2What can each agent reach and do?

Ask forAccess of the ten most powerful agents, and why each needs it.
StrongA one-line written scope for each, reviewed this quarter.
Weak"It uses the employee's login", which means it reaches everything that person can.
Our answerNumberOwner

3What happens automatically in the first ten minutes?

Ask forThe list of pre-approved actions, such as locking an account, ending a session, isolating a machine.
StrongA short written list, each action with limits and an approver.
Weak"The analyst escalates", with no list.
Our answerNumberOwner

4Can we stop one agent without stopping the business?

Ask forTime to cut off a single agent, and who can do it.
StrongA named role, a tested procedure, and a measured time.
Weak"We would disable the user", when the agent holds its own credentials.
Our answerMinutesOwner

5When did we last practise this, and what broke?

Ask forThe last exercise and the one thing that failed.
StrongA recent drill, a real failure, and what changed afterwards.
Weak"No issues found", or no exercise at all.
Our answerDateOwner
