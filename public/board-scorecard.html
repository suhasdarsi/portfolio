Five questions on AI agent security

Ask the same five every quarter. Write what you were told, then tick how strong the answer was.

1How many agents run here, and who owns each?

Ask for The count found running, with an owner per entry. Not a list of approved tools. Strong A recent inventory from real devices and networks, with owner and date. Weak A survey, a policy, or approved tools only.

2What can each agent reach and do?

Ask for Access of the ten most powerful agents, and why each needs it. Strong A one-line written scope each, reviewed this quarter. Weak “It uses the employee’s login.”

3What happens automatically in the first ten minutes?

Ask for The list of pre-approved actions (lock an account, end a session, isolate a machine). Strong A short written list, each action with limits and an approver. Weak “The analyst escalates,” with no list.

4Can we stop one agent without stopping the business?

Ask for Time to cut off a single agent, and who can do it. Strong A named role, a tested procedure, a measured time. Weak “We would disable the user.”

5When did we last practise this, and what broke?

Ask for The last exercise and the one thing that failed. Strong A recent drill, a real failure, what changed after. Weak “No issues found,” or no exercise.

A good answer has a number, a date and an owner. A weak one has reassurance. Full note: suhasdarsi.com/notes/questions-boards-should-ask-ai-agent-security
