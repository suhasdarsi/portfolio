## How the market is shaped

- **Commercial intelligence providers** (Recorded Future, Flashpoint, Intel 471) sell curated reporting on attackers, the dark web and fraud.
- **Internet scanning services** (Shodan, Censys, GreyNoise) map every exposed host and every source of scanning noise.
- **Analysis services** (VirusTotal, ANY.RUN, Hybrid Analysis, urlscan.io) check files and links, and are often free for basic use.
- **Open source and community** (MISP, OpenCTI, abuse.ch, MITRE ATT&CK) underpin most programmes.

## Where the overlap is

Intelligence is increasingly sold as a feature of SIEM, EDR and email products rather than on its own. Breach data overlaps with identity security, because leaked credentials lead straight to account takeover. Internet scanning overlaps with attack surface management.

## Consolidation

Mastercard bought Recorded Future. Google owns VirusTotal and Mandiant. Large platforms keep buying intelligence companies to feed their own detections.

## How to buy

Use the free sources first. Pay for intelligence only when someone has time to act on it, and judge it by how many of your own decisions it changes, not by how many feeds it adds.
