## How the market is shaped

- **Compliance automation** (Vanta, Drata, Secureframe, Sprinto, Thoropass, Scrut, and open-source Comp AI) dominates the startup and mid-market segment. These tools are mostly bought to get SOC 2 or ISO 27001.
- **Enterprise GRC suites** (OneTrust, Optro, formerly AuditBoard) serve large risk, audit and privacy teams.
- **Security ratings and third-party risk** (SecurityScorecard, BitSight, UpGuard, Whistic) assess vendors from the outside.
- **Questionnaire tools** (Conveyor) use AI to answer customers’ security reviews.

## Where the overlap is

Compliance automation vendors now bundle vendor risk, trust centres and AI questionnaire answering, so a single contract covers both directions of a security review. AI has also become a compliance topic in its own right: ISO 42001, the EU AI Act and customer AI questionnaires are now part of most GRC products.

## Consolidation

Drata bought SafeBase to add a trust centre. The compliance automation market is crowded enough that further mergers seem likely, while open-source options like Comp AI put pressure on prices.

## How to buy

Pick based on the frameworks you need and the integrations you already run. Evidence collection only works if the tool connects to your stack. Check what renewal pricing looks like, because initial discounts in this market are steep.
