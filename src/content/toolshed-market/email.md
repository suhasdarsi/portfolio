## How the market is shaped

- **Long-standing gateways** (Proofpoint, Mimecast) sit in front of the mailbox. They are full-featured and widely deployed.
- **API-based tools** (Abnormal, Sublime Security, Material Security, Ironscales, Check Point Harmony Email) connect directly to Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace and use behaviour to catch business email compromise, the kind of attack that contains no link or attachment.
- **DMARC specialists** (Valimail, dmarcian, EasyDMARC, Red Sift) help you reach enforcement on your own domains.
- **Awareness training** (KnowBe4, plus open-source GoPhish) trains people and runs simulated phishing.

## Where the overlap is

Every email vendor now sells awareness training, and every awareness vendor sells some filtering. Microsoft’s built-in filtering keeps improving, which pushes third-party vendors toward catching what Microsoft misses rather than replacing it. Email security also overlaps with data protection, through outbound DLP and misdirected-email prevention.

## Consolidation

Proofpoint bought Tessian and Hornetsecurity, and has agreed to buy Varonis. KnowBe4 bought Egress. Mimecast bought Code42. Email is consolidating into wider “human risk” platforms.

## How to buy

Run an API-based tool in monitor mode for a few weeks and see what it catches that your current filtering lets through. That is the clearest test. Separately, get DMARC to enforcement: it is cheap, and it protects your customers too.
