## How the market is shaped

- **SASE and SSE platforms** (Zscaler, Netskope, Prisma Access, Cato Networks, Cloudflare) deliver web security, private access and data controls from the cloud.
- **Firewall vendors** (Fortinet, along with Palo Alto Networks and Check Point) still sell hardware, and have built their own SASE services alongside it.
- **Zero-trust access and mesh VPNs** (Tailscale, Twingate, NetBird, Firezone, Pomerium) are simple to deploy and popular with engineering teams.
- **Open source** (WireGuard, OPNsense, pfSense, Pi-hole, CrowdSec, Cilium) is a strong base layer.

## Where the overlap is

SASE platforms now include DLP, browser security, CASB and AI security. Many buyers get those features through their network vendor rather than buying them separately. Edge vendors (Akamai, Cloudflare) span both public-facing protection and workforce access. The firewall and SASE markets are converging as firewall vendors move to the cloud.

## Consolidation

Cato Networks bought Aim Security to add AI security to its platform. Akamai bought LayerX for browser security and Noname for API security. The SASE leaders are buying in adjacent areas rather than buying each other.

## How to buy

For a small or engineering-led team, a mesh VPN plus DNS filtering covers most needs. Larger organisations usually standardise on one SASE platform. Test performance from where your people actually work, and check what the platform can see of AI traffic.
