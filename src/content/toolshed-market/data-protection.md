## How the market is shaped

- **DSPM vendors** (Cyera, BigID, Sentra, Varonis, Concentric AI, Securiti) find and classify sensitive data across cloud, SaaS and on-premises storage.
- **DLP vendors** (Nightfall, Cyberhaven, MIND, Strac, Microsoft Purview) control data as it moves. Newer vendors use content and lineage rather than old regex rules.
- **Backup vendors** (Rubrik, Cohesity) have moved into security, because they already hold a copy of everything.
- **Encryption** is split between free tools for individuals and products for sharing and storing sensitive fields.

## Where the overlap is

DSPM and DLP are converging: knowing where data is and controlling where it goes are two halves of one job. The main driver is AI. Every vendor now pitches stopping sensitive data from reaching AI tools, and getting AI-ready data governance in place before Copilot-style assistants can read everything. Data protection also overlaps with SASE, browser and email security, which all have DLP features.

## Consolidation

Proofpoint agreed in October 2026 to buy Varonis for about $6.3 billion, expected to close in 2027. Rubrik bought Laminar to add DSPM. Mimecast bought Code42. Data security startups are some of the most active buyers and targets in security.

## How to buy

Find where sensitive data lives before you buy controls for how it moves. Ask how accurate classification is on your own data, and how each product handles AI tools: browser chatbots, coding agents and copilots that read your SaaS data.
