Phishing, invoice fraud and malicious attachments still arrive by email more than any other way. Protecting email has three parts.

## How it breaks down

- **Email protection** filters incoming mail. Traditional gateways sit in front of the mailbox. Newer API-based tools connect to Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace and use behaviour to catch impersonation that has no malicious link at all.
- **Domain authentication (DMARC)** sets up SPF, DKIM and DMARC so nobody can send mail that looks like it came from your domain, and reports who tries.
- **Awareness and phishing tests** train people to spot what the filters miss, and measure how often they click.

## Where to start

Get DMARC to enforcement. It is cheap and protects your customers as well as your staff. Then add API-based protection on top of your mail provider’s built-in filtering.
