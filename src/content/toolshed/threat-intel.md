Threat intelligence adds outside context to what you see inside. It answers “is this IP, file or domain known?” and “what are attackers targeting right now?”

## How it breaks down

- **Intelligence platforms** collect and curate reporting on attackers and their tools, and feed it into your detections.
- **Internet scanning** services search every exposed host on the internet, including yours. They show what an attacker sees first.
- **Malware and URL analysis** checks a file, hash or link against many engines, or runs it in a sandbox.
- **Breach data** shows which of your accounts and credentials have appeared in leaks.
- **Frameworks** like MITRE ATT&CK give everyone a shared vocabulary for attacker techniques.

## Where to start

The free services go a long way: check your own exposure on internet search engines, monitor your domains for breached credentials, and use ATT&CK to see which techniques your detections cover. Paid intelligence pays off once someone has time to act on it.
