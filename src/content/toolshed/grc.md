GRC tools don’t stop attacks. They prove you have controls, track the risks you have accepted, and answer the questions customers ask before they buy.

## How it breaks down

- **Compliance automation** connects to your cloud, identity and HR systems, collects evidence continuously, and maps it to frameworks like SOC 2 and ISO 27001.
- **Risk and governance platforms** are enterprise suites for risk registers, internal audit, privacy programmes and policy management.
- **Third-party risk** rates your vendors’ security from the outside and manages their assessments.
- **Security questionnaires** answer customers’ security reviews from a library of approved answers.

## Where to start

Most startups begin with compliance automation when the first enterprise customer asks for SOC 2. Add third-party risk and questionnaire tools once vendor reviews start piling up in both directions.
