Every user, every credential and most attacks pass through an endpoint. It is also where AI agents now run, which makes the endpoint a key control point for AI use too.

## How it breaks down

- **Endpoint detection and response (EDR)** is an agent on each device that detects malware and hands-on-keyboard attackers, and can isolate a machine with one click.
- **Device management and visibility** (MDM and UEM) keeps devices enrolled, configured, encrypted and patched, and lets you ask what every machine is running.
- **Application control** allows only approved software to run. It is very effective, but it takes work to maintain.
- **Mac utilities** are small free tools that show what launches, persists and connects out on a Mac.

## Where to start

EDR and device management are the baseline: you can’t protect a device you don’t manage. Add application control on high-risk machines, and an inventory of AI tools and agents on top.
