These are the tools penetration testers and red teams use. Use them only on systems you own or have written permission to test.

## How it breaks down

- **Web testing** intercepts and replays web traffic to find flaws in applications.
- **Recon and scanning** maps hosts, subdomains, open ports and known weaknesses.
- **Exploitation and command and control** frameworks exploit systems and control them during an authorised engagement.
- **Active Directory and internal networks** tools find attack paths through Windows domains.
- **Password cracking** recovers passwords from hashes to show how weak they are.
- **Reverse engineering** takes binaries and apps apart.
- **Automated pentesting** runs attacks continuously instead of once a year.
- **Adversary emulation** replays known attacker techniques to check that your detections actually fire.
- **Bug bounty platforms** pay outside researchers for what they find.
- **Distros and hardware** bundle the tools together.

## Where to start

For defenders, adversary emulation and automated pentesting give the most value: they show whether your controls work, not just whether they are switched on.
